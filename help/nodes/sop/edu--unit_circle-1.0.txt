= EDU Unit Circle =

"""Shows the usual unit circle properties while the user moves a point attached to it."""



WARNING:
    This node is made for education purposes only. You can use it for other things, but at your own risks.

The user can move a point attached to the unit circle while seeing the different values evolve on the screen.

To enter manipulation mode, move the mouse over the viewport and press ((Enter))

@parameters

    Position:
        #id: t
        The position of the point on the circle.
